Spatial data for your GIS   
===============================   

## About   

Get up-to-date OpenStreetMap data in a convenient format using data.nextgis.com   

Source:   
   * OpenStreetMap (https://osm.org) – database of open spatial data and a community of mappers who keep it up-to-date.  


## Contents   

Each dataset contains:  
   * layers of geodata (data folder)  
   * symbol icons for QGIS legend (svg folder)  
   * ready-made project file for your GIS (only for QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, ArcMap and MapInfo formats)
   * order boundary (order_boundary.geojson)
